Logic Technology Announces New Partnership with ARIANNA

News dated September 19, 2026 · Estimated read time: 3 minutes

Back to resources
Partner announcement

Logic Technology has officially added ARIANNA to its portfolio. Effective September 2026, ARIANNA becomes available to Logic Technology's customers across Benelux, giving engineering teams a single workspace to manage Software and Hardware Bills of Materials (SBOMs/HBOMs), monitor vulnerabilities continuously, and simplify reporting under the EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA).

“Manufacturers of connected products are being asked to prove far more than that their software works, they need to show they know what's in it and can act fast when a new vulnerability emerges. Our partnership with Logic Technology brings that capability directly to engineering teams who need it most, backed by local expertise they already trust.”

Isabella Donders, CEO ARIANNA

Managing vulnerabilities across the product lifecycle

ARIANNA turns vulnerability data into a manageable operational process. ARIANNA supports engineering teams in building trusted inventories with accurate SBOMs and HBOMs, monitoring vulnerabilities continuously with EU-hosted data residency, and prioritizing vulnerabilities based on exploitability and product impact. Remediation is coordinated with assigned owners and deadlines, and every decision is backed by VEX generation and audit-ready compliance reporting, with API integration into existing engineering workflows. In short: teams understand what products are affected, decide what matters now, assign the next step, and retain the evidence behind each decision.

For Logic Technology's customer base, engineering teams in defense, aerospace, medical devices, industrial and IoT, the addition of ARIANNA extends the company's security offering, giving customers a single, auditable workspace for vulnerability management throughout the product lifecycle.

“Vulnerability management used to be a periodic exercise. With ARIANNA, it becomes part of the daily engineering process, teams know exactly what's in their products, what needs attention, and can prove it. Adding ARIANNA to our portfolio means we can support customers not just at the moment of a scan, but throughout the full lifecycle of their product.”

Gevorg Melikdjanjan, Solution Expert Logic Technology
