ARIANNA Announces New Partnership with COMPLYD

News dated May 29, 2026 · Estimated read time: 3 minutes

Back to resources
Partner announcement

ARIANNA announces a new partnership with COMPLYD, strengthening the connection between Cyber Resilience Act governance and the technical evidence needed to manage software and hardware component risk.

COMPLYD supports organizations in navigating the compliance requirements of the Cyber Resilience Act. ARIANNA complements that governance work with the SBOM, HBOM, and vulnerability management capabilities needed to maintain visibility across connected products and long-lived device portfolios.

The cooperation helps manufacturers link regulatory readiness with operational security data: component inventories, vulnerability relevance, triage workflows, and the evidence needed to support product-security decisions.

“At COMPLYD, we help organizations navigate the compliance requirements of the Cyber Resilience Act, but CRA compliance also demands deep visibility into every software and hardware component. That is exactly where ARIANNA excels. Their SBOM and vulnerability management platform fills a critical technical layer that complements our governance approach perfectly.”

Stephan Hutterer, CEO COMPLYD.IO

The partnership reflects ARIANNA's continued focus on building an ecosystem around embedded device security, vulnerability monitoring, and compliance management for connected products and industrial environments.

“COMPLYD brings strong CRA governance expertise to the market. Together, we can help organizations connect compliance programs with the technical visibility required to act on component and vulnerability risk,” said Jan Jager, CEO of ARIANNA.

For more information about ARIANNA and our partner ecosystem, visit ariannateam.ai/partners.
