ARIANNA Expands into Asia Through New Partnership with REEKON

News dated May 11, 2026 · Estimated read time: 4 minutes

Back to resources
Partner announcement

ARIANNA is proud to announce a new strategic partnership with REEKON, a fast-growing cybersecurity and compliance specialist with offices across Singapore, Taiwan, India, and Hong Kong. Through this collaboration, REEKON will join the ARIANNA ecosystem as an official Implementation Partner for the Asian market.

This partnership marks an important milestone in ARIANNA's international growth strategy and strengthens our ability to support manufacturers of connected and embedded devices across Asia with continuous vulnerability management and Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) readiness.

REEKON brings extensive expertise in cybersecurity compliance, payment security, penetration testing, architecture validation, and certification support. The company has built a strong reputation in Asia for helping organizations design, certify, and maintain secure products and solutions.

Implementation partner support

As an ARIANNA Implementation Partner, REEKON will support customers with:

  • ARIANNA onboarding and deployment
  • Device model creation (HBOM/SBOM)
  • Vulnerability management processes
  • Compliance preparation for regulations such as the CRA
  • Security advisory and implementation support
  • Integration of ARIANNA into existing product security workflows

The collaboration combines REEKON's regional presence and compliance expertise with ARIANNA's continuous vulnerability monitoring platform for connected devices and embedded systems.

With increasing cybersecurity regulations emerging worldwide, manufacturers are facing growing pressure to maintain visibility over vulnerabilities in their products throughout the entire lifecycle. The European Cyber Resilience Act in particular is driving demand for structured vulnerability management, SBOM governance, and faster incident response capabilities.

By partnering with REEKON, ARIANNA can now better support organizations in Asia that develop IoT devices, embedded systems, industrial products, payment solutions, and connected technologies that must comply with evolving international cybersecurity requirements.

"Asia is an extremely important market for connected device manufacturers and cybersecurity innovation," said Jan Jager, CEO of ARIANNA. "REEKON has strong technical expertise, deep knowledge of cybersecurity certification processes, and an excellent regional network. Together we can help manufacturers improve product security maturity and prepare for the next generation of cybersecurity regulations."

REEKON has been actively involved in cybersecurity workshops and initiatives focused on AI security, quantum-safe technologies, IoT security, and evolving cybersecurity standards across Asia.

This partnership is another step in ARIANNA's mission to become the leading vulnerability management platform for connected devices and embedded systems worldwide.
