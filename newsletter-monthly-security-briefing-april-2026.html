ARIANNA — Vulnerability Management for Connected Devices

Edition: April 2026

From SBOM to continuous risk monitoring — built for embedded, IoT and regulated environments.

ARIANNA is now a standalone company

We're excited to announce that ARIANNA is now a separate company, spun out of Security Pattern.

This step reflects the growing demand for a dedicated solution that addresses one of the hardest challenges in cybersecurity today: managing vulnerabilities in connected products across their full lifecycle.

What this means for you: a fully focused product organization, faster development and innovation cycles, stronger alignment with customer needs, and a growing partner ecosystem.

ARIANNA remains built on the deep cybersecurity expertise of Security Pattern — now with a sharper mission and dedicated roadmap.

We're just getting started

Why traditional vulnerability management doesn't work for IoT

Most vulnerability management tools were designed for IT environments.

But connected products are different: long lifecycles, complex supply chains, embedded software and hardware dependencies, and regulatory pressure from frameworks such as CRA, UNECE, and RED all change the problem.

That's why ARIANNA focuses on device-centric modeling with HBOM and SBOM, continuous vulnerability monitoring, and context-based risk analysis.

Built for products — not just infrastructure

What's new in ARIANNA

We've introduced several improvements to make onboarding and usage more effective: improved Device Model creation flow, streamlined SBOM ingestion via ARIANNA SCA tools, enhanced vulnerability tracking and filtering, and better reporting for compliance and stakeholders.

The result is faster time-to-value, higher adoption during trials, and better insights for engineering and security teams.

From SBOM to continuous risk visibility

Creating an SBOM is just the starting point.

ARIANNA enables continuous monitoring of vulnerabilities over time, impact analysis per device or product line, and clear prioritization of remediation actions.

This is critical for CRA compliance readiness, product security governance, and long-term lifecycle management. Security doesn't stop after release — neither should your visibility.
