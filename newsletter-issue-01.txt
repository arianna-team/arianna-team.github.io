1. ARIANNA IS NOW A STANDALONE COMPANY
We're excited to announce that ARIANNA is now a separate company, spun out of Security Pattern.

This step reflects growing demand for a dedicated solution that addresses one of the hardest challenges in cybersecurity today: managing vulnerabilities in connected products across their full lifecycle.

What this means for you:
- A fully focused product organization
- Faster development and innovation cycles
- Stronger alignment with customer needs
- A growing partner ecosystem

ARIANNA remains built on the deep cybersecurity expertise of Security Pattern — now with a sharper mission and a dedicated roadmap.

2. WHY TRADITIONAL VULNERABILITY MANAGEMENT DOESN'T WORK FOR IOT
Most vulnerability management tools were designed for IT environments. Connected products are different: they have long lifecycles, complex supply chains, embedded software and hardware dependencies, and growing regulatory pressure from frameworks such as CRA, UNECE, and RED.

That's why ARIANNA focuses on device-centric modeling with HBOM and SBOM, continuous vulnerability monitoring, and context-based risk analysis. It is built for products, not just infrastructure.

3. WHAT'S NEW IN ARIANNA
We've introduced several improvements to make onboarding and day-to-day usage more effective, including an improved Device Model creation flow, streamlined SBOM ingestion through ARIANNA SCA tools, enhanced vulnerability tracking and filtering, and better reporting for compliance and stakeholder communication.

The result is faster time-to-value, stronger trial adoption, and clearer insights for engineering and security teams.

4. FROM SBOM TO CONTINUOUS RISK VISIBILITY
Creating an SBOM is only the starting point. ARIANNA extends that foundation with continuous monitoring over time, impact analysis by device or product line, and clear prioritization of remediation actions.

This matters for CRA readiness, product security governance, and long-term lifecycle management. Security does not stop after release, and visibility should not either.
